Mam Tor at sunrise. Got up at 5 AM to catch the light. Worth it.
← Previous Photo 1 of 12 Next →

Shot this at sunrise on the second day of the trip. The weather forecast said it would be cloudy all weekend but we got lucky with a brief window of clear sky. Had to hike up in the dark which was a bit sketchy but the view at the top made it worthwhile.

This was frame 8 on the roll. I bracketed a few shots but this one had the best balance between the sky and the foreground. Portra 400 handles the early morning light really well.
